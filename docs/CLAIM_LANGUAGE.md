# Claim Language Rules

## Forbidden without separate evidence

- every payload is verified / guaranteed
- immutable ledger / immutable execution
- non-repudiation
- factual truth
- AI safety (complete)
- EU AI Act / ISO certified (unless independent conformity)
- Merkle proof (unless Merkle contract implemented)
- production ready
- zero-drift (universal)
- side-effect free (without purity contract)

## Allowed bounded forms

- tamper-evident SHA-256 hash chain (when verified)
- contracted admission boundary (when tested against real implementation)
- deterministic canonical serialization under a frozen contract
- fail-closed HALT on specified failures

## Status vocabulary

DOCUMENTED | IMPLEMENTED | TESTED | VERIFIED | SEALED | PLANNED | BLOCKED | HISTORICAL_CLAIM_UNVERIFIED | NOT_CLAIMED

Do not mark `[x]` for planned or partial work.
